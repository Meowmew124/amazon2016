# Amazon 2016

A userscript that makes amazon.com look like it did in 2016.

## Install

1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) or [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/).
2. Open [`amazon2016.user.js`](amazon2016.user.js), click **Raw**, and your userscript manager will offer to install it.

## What it changes (v0.1)

- **Header:** single navy bar, "Try Prime" under the logo, Prime promo on the right.
- **Second row:** Deliver to · Departments ▾ · Your Amazon.com · Today's Deals · Gift Cards & Registry · Sell · Help, with Hello. Sign in / Account & Lists · Orders · Try Prime ▾ · Cart on the right.
- **Search bar:** 2016 sizing and colors.
- **Product pages:** Arial, smaller title, flat red "Price:", yellow/orange gradient Add to Cart and Buy Now buttons, "customer reviews" wording.
- **Everywhere:** Arial font, `#0066c0` links, 2016 buttons and boxes.
